\chapter{Exploratory Results}\label{app:exploratory}
These tables report the two exploratory rounds that preceded the third pre-registration (\cref{sec:s3-explore}). They are \tierE{}: the first round compared 14 arms (S30 among them) with the reference A1 and the second 10 new arms next to A1 and S30; intervals are the repository's $97.5\%$ paired-bootstrap intervals, and the best of many arms is subject to a winner's curse. Variant names: S$f$ = kept fraction $f$ in percent; \code{w}$n$ = warm-up of $n$ steps (default 100); \code{gn} = rows ranked by the last-layer gradient norm; \code{OSGD} = kept fraction following the default rule of Ordered SGD; \code{up} = kept fraction rising from $0.1$ to $0.5$; \code{k} = gradient divided by the number of kept rows; SB2, SB3 = Selective-Backprop with $\beta=2,3$ and the baseline's hyper-parameters; \code{p100} = priming of 100 updates. FLOPs of SB are the uncached count.

\paragraph{What these tables do and do not show.} Removing the warm-up (S20w0, S15w0, and warm-ups of 5 and 10 steps) gives the largest and the most consistent advantages over S30 and S20 in both rounds; warm-up 25 gives clearly less; lower fractions help by a few percent to about $13\%$ against S30; ranking by gradient norm, the Ordered-SGD schedule, a rising fraction and the rescaled gradient do not give a clear further gain. Because 14 arms were compared in the first round and the best was singled out, the first-round point estimates are optimistic, which is why the confirmatory test of Study~3 uses fresh seeds.
